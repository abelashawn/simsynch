From briefing room to signed debrief in one session.

SimSynch follows the actual rhythm of a simulator check — not an abstraction of it. Every step maps directly to what an instructor does in the bay.

A
Build the session plan
Configure a 4-slot template or use the generator: select phase, degree of difficulty, role focus, and scenario category. SimSynch draws from a 125-event A320 scenario bank and attaches the right Observable Behaviours automatically via a three-tier OB matching engine.
125 scenarios · Phase 1–8 · DOD 1–3
B
Upload an existing programme
Have an operator lesson plan, FCOM abnormal procedures document, or LPC/OPC Instructor Guide? Upload the PDF. SimSynch auto-detects the format, extracts training events, and maps each to the built-in exercise library. You pick which exercises to evaluate.
FCOM · Lesson Plan · Instructor Guide

The simulator's own Instructor Operating Station — SimSynch™ runs alongside it, on a separate device, without touching sim control.

C
Grade in real time
The ORCA grading view shows one Observable Behaviour per row. Mark it observed, select a grade from 1–5, add a note. Every entry saves immediately — no batch submit, no lost data if the session runs long. The controlling grade (lowest observed OB) updates live.
Auto-save · Controlling grade · MAL tracking
D
Debrief with evidence
The Session Debrief shows a live competency radar, bar chart, and Venn diagram built from the graded OBs. Focus areas (below-MAL competencies) are highlighted automatically. Save the debrief to mark the session Complete and lock the record.
Competency radar · PASS / RETRAIN verdict

What changes when you use SimSynch™.

Concrete improvements in how simulator instruction is prepared, delivered, and documented.

🗂
No more blank paper forms
Observable Behaviour checklists are generated automatically from the selected scenario, not handwritten or printed the night before. The right OBs for each scenario are attached before the session starts.

Grade during the session, not after
Every grade saves the instant you click. Notes save on blur. No "Save" button to forget. When the session ends, the debrief record is already complete — you're reviewing it, not building it from memory.
Real-time Supabase persistence · ORCA controlling-grade rule applied live
📄
Upload any operator programme
LPC/OPC Instructor Guide, FCOM abnormal procedures PDF, or EBT lesson plan — SimSynch auto-detects the format, extracts the training events, and maps them. Three document formats supported with no manual configuration.

Competency evidence, not just grades
The debrief shows a radar chart, bar graph, and Venn diagram of competency coverage. Each chart is built from the OBs you observed, not averaged from a single overall score. The evidence is there before you open your mouth in the debrief.

Scenario bank with 125 A320 events
The scenario selector shows every event in your Scenarios.csv bank, filterable by phase, DOD, and category. Competency coverage gaps in the current session plan drive the match score — the most useful scenario for this crew is ranked first.

EASA structure, not an afterthought
MAL threshold = 3 throughout. Any competency graded below 3 triggers RETRAIN regardless of overall average — the ORCA controlling-grade principle. Session structure is validated against AMC1 ORO.FC.231 before you commit to it.
