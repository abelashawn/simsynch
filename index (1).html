A320 full-flight simulator session, dusk approach profile

From programme upload to signed debrief in one platform.

SimSynch follows the actual rhythm of a simulator check — not an abstraction of it. Every step maps directly to what an instructor does in the bay.

A
Inject the programme PDF
Upload any simulator programme document — FCOM abnormal procedures, EBT lesson plan, or Instructor Guide / LPC-OPC. SimSynch parses it, extracts training event titles, and matches them against the exercise library automatically.
FCOM · Lesson Plan · Instructor Guide
B
Select exercises, arrange the days
Matched exercises land on a visual two-day board, pre-sorted into the correct phase lane. Drag any exercise between days, set sequence within a lane, and assign crew role — CM1, CM2, Both, or Eval — per exercise.
Day 1 / Day 2 board · CM1 / CM2 roles
C
Grade in real time, per pilot
The ORCA grading view shows one Observable Behaviour per row, graded separately for CM1 and CM2 on the OM-D scale. Mark it observed, grade it, add a note. Every entry saves immediately — no batch submit, no lost data if the session runs long.
Auto-save · Per-pilot grading · MAL tracking
D
Debrief, sign off, export
The Session Debrief shows a live competency radar built from the graded OBs. Focus areas (below-MAL competencies) are flagged automatically. The Head of Training approves or returns the session; approved records lock.
